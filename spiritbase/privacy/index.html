Privacy Policy

Spiritbase · Effective November 17, 2026

Spiritbase doesn't collect any information about you. Your bar is kept on your device and in your own iCloud, and we never see it.

Your data is yours.

Your ingredients, recipes, photos and barcodes are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they sync through your own iCloud account to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple stores that data under your account and its own privacy policy. We have no access to it. Spiritbase has no accounts or sign-in of its own, and we don't run any servers.

You can also export and delete your data. “Export my data” saves a copy of your whole bar, photos included. “Erase all my data” deletes every recipe, ingredient, photo and barcode from all of your devices and from iCloud. Links you've already shared keep working for up to 30 days.

Your data is not ours.

Spiritbase has no ads, analytics or tracking. We can't sell or share your data with anyone because we don't have it. We aren't looking at your bar by design.

Other privacy concerns.

Camera and photos
With your permission, Spiritbase uses the camera to scan barcodes, photograph bottles and drinks, and to read recipes from photos. You can also add pictures and recipes from your photo library. Spiritbase can only access the photos you choose. Reading text from a photo happens on your device. And, if you change your mind, you can turn off camera access any time.
Importing recipes
When you import a recipe from the internet, your device loads that page straight from the website. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Spiritbase uses Apple's on-device model to pick out the recipe. Recipes you send to Spiritbase from other apps through the Share Sheet or Shortcuts are handled the same way.
Searching for a cocktail
When searching, Spiritbase uses DuckDuckGo in a private browser window inside the app that keeps nothing between searches.
Sharing
You choose what to share and who gets it. A shared recipe or ingredient travels as a file through Messages, AirDrop or anything else you pick. If you share a link instead, a copy is kept in your own iCloud, anyone with the link can view it, and Spiritbase deletes the copy after 30 days (the next time you open the app). Whoever receives a shared item gets their own copy.
Apple
If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us crash reports and basic usage statistics. We use them only to fix problems in the app.

Children.

We don't want children using our home bartending app. Spiritbase is age-restricted, but only for that reason.

Questions.

Spiritbase is made by Ubiquitous Dingus LLC. Questions about privacy: brian@ubiquitousdingus.com. If you email us, we use your address only to reply, and we'll delete your messages if you ask. If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
