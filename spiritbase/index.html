Spiritbase

In testing, not on the App Store yet

An app for your home bar. Save recipes. Know what's on your shelf. See what you can make right now.

Keep track of the bottles, mixers and garnishes on your shelf. Save cocktail recipes from a webpage, photo, or text (or enter them manually). Know at a glance which drinks you can make with what you have, and which drinks you are just a few ingredients away from making. Share recipes and bottles, with or without photos, with friends.

Spiritbase keeps everything on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We don't have our own sign-in and we never see your data. Requires iOS 26 or later, on iPhone or iPad.

Common questions

Where is my bar kept?
On your device and in your own iCloud account. There are no accounts to create, and nothing about you or your bar is sent to Ubiquitous Dingus.
Will my bar be on my new iPhone or my iPad?
Yes, when they're signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Spiritbase syncs through your own iCloud.
Which devices does Spiritbase work on?
iPhone with iOS 26 or later (iPhone 11 or newer) and iPad with iPadOS 26 or later. Spiritbase will work on iPhone Duo.
