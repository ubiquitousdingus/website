Better Reminders Widget

Coming to the App Store

See how your day is going at a glance. Better Reminders Widget shows your Apple Reminders Today list as a ring that closes as you finish, like the Activity rings.

The ring lives on Apple Watch complications and on iPhone and iPad Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets. Check reminders off on your watch or in the app and the ring fills in. Your tasks stay in Apple Reminders: the app reads your Today list and marks reminders complete there.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 27 or later, and watchOS 26 or later for the Apple Watch app.

Common questions

Which reminders count toward the ring?
The same ones as Apple Reminders' Today list: reminders in any list that are due today or overdue. Reminders with no due date, or due later, aren't counted. A day with nothing due shows a full ring.
Why won't my ring close?
Overdue reminders count until they're done, just as they stay in Apple's Today list. Complete them or give them a new date and the ring can close.
My widget is a few minutes behind Reminders.
iOS decides how often widgets refresh, so a short delay after a change in Reminders is normal. Opening the app updates them at once. For faster updates, the app's Widgets screen explains an optional Shortcuts automation.
How do I change what a widget shows or opens?
Touch and hold the widget, then choose Edit Widget. Each widget can show the number of reminders left, the percentage done, or just the ring, and can open this app or Apple Reminders.
Why does the app ask for access to my reminders?
It needs to read your Today list to draw the ring and to mark reminders complete when you check them off. There are no accounts, and your reminders aren't sent to Ubiquitous Dingus.
