Privacy Policy

Better Reminders Widget · Effective November 17, 2026

Better Reminders Widget doesn't collect any information about you. Your reminders stay in Apple Reminders, and we never see them.

Your data is yours.

When you use Better Reminders Widget, everything happens on your device and syncs through Apple's Reminders app via your iCloud account. We have no access to your reminders, so we cannot see them and do not know anything about them. Better Reminders Widget has no accounts or sign-in, and we don't run any servers.

The order you give your Today list and the choices you make for each widget are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes them, and your reminders stay in Apple Reminders.

Your data is not ours.

Better Reminders Widget has no ads, analytics or tracking. We can't sell or share your data with anyone because we don't have it. We aren't looking at your reminders by design.

Other privacy concerns.

Reminders access
The app asks for access to your reminders because it can't draw the ring without them. If you change your mind, you can turn off Reminders access any time. The ring can't update until you turn it back on.
iPhone and Apple Watch
The app passes your Today list and your check-offs between your own iPhone and Apple Watch, over Apple's connection between the two.
Apple
If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us crash reports and basic usage statistics. We use them only to fix problems in the app.

Questions.

Better Reminders Widget is made by Ubiquitous Dingus LLC. Questions about privacy: brian@ubiquitousdingus.com. If you email us, we use your address only to reply, and we'll delete your messages if you ask. If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
